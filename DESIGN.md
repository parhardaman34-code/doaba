## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an environment of clinical precision, calm assurance, and progressive dental care. Built to reduce patient apprehension surrounding complex dental and implant procedures, the visual language merges Swiss-inspired layout discipline with organic, humanistic comfort.

### Philosophy & Visual Tone
- **Precision Care**: Interfaces communicate hygiene, high surgical capability, and advanced dental technology through crisp structure, generous negative space, and uncluttered views.
- **Approachable Reassurance**: Technical dental data is softened through warm, humanist geometric curves, eliminating the harsh, cold sterility often felt in traditional medical software.
- **Clarity of Information**: High visual hierarchy ensures patients effortlessly navigate treatment plans, appointment booking, pricing transparency, and post-operative instructions.

### Visual Style
A hybrid of **Modern Medical Minimalist** and **Subtle Tonal Layering**. The style relies on pure white surfaces, soft slate-tinted canvas backdrops, low-contrast inner borders, and cool cerulean-tinted ambient glows rather than dense, murky drop shadows.

## Colors

The palette leverages restorative oceanic and medical tones to induce calm and convey clinical integrity.

### Color Architecture
- **Primary (`#0284c7` - Cerulean Cyan)**: Represents advanced medical technology, reliability, and precision. Used for primary calls to action, interactive highlights, and active navigation nodes.
- **Secondary (`#0d9488` - Surgical Teal)**: Evokes hygiene, wellness, and therapeutic safety. Used for secondary actions, confirmed states, clinical badging, and trust markers.
- **Tertiary (`#14b8a6` - Soothing Mint Accent)**: Bright, energetic accent deployed sparingly for positive health milestones, recovery notifications, and featured badges.
- **Neutral Core (`#334155` - Slate Slate)**: Deep slate tone ensuring high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Surface & Functional Roles
- **Canvas Base**: `#f8fafc` (Slate 50) provides a soft, glare-free background.
- **Surface Layer 1**: `#ffffff` (Pure White) serves as the elevated interactive card and dialogue standard.
- **Surface Layer 2 (Subtle Recess)**: `#f1f5f9` (Slate 100) used for input backgrounds, segmented controls, and inactive table alternate rows.
- **Borders & Dividers**: Low-contrast hairpins using `#e2e8f0` (Slate 200). Never exceed 1px stroke weight.
- **Semantic Feedback**:
  - Success / Healed: `#059669` (Emerald 600)
  - Warning / Preparation: `#d97706` (Amber 600)
  - Critical / Contraindication: `#e11d48` (Rose 600)

## Typography

Typography is set exclusively in **Plus Jakarta Sans** to unify contemporary technical discipline with rounded, reassuring terminals that build patient trust.

### Hierarchy & Usage Rules
- **Display & Headlines**: Apply tight negative tracking (`-0.01em` to `-0.03em`) to larger display cuts to preserve optical compactness. Reserve `display-lg` strictly for hero statements, landing page value propositions, and primary marketing statistics.
- **Body Text**: Maintain standard body weights (`400`) at comfortable line heights (1.5x minimum) to reduce eye fatigue during reading of medical terms, post-care guides, and consent documentation.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Ensure uppercase transformation on `label-sm` when applied to technical badge indicators, medical status tokens, or table headers, accompanied by wide letter spacing (`0.04em`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on a standard **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and tablet screens, shifting to a single- or two-column flow on mobile viewports.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (1280px+)**: 12 columns, `margin: 3rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Maximum content container width capped at `1280px` for optimal reading spans.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8 columns, `margin-tablet: 2rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `margin-mobile: 1.25rem`, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spatial Rhythm
- Interior component padding uses strict 8-point increments (`space-xs`, `space-md`, `space-lg`, `space-xl`).
- High-level sections (e.g., service listings, doctor credentials, implant treatment phases) must maintain spacious separation via `space-3xl` to preserve an open, clean clinical atmosphere.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic medical laboratory feel, this system avoids high-contrast drops, heavy skeuomorphism, and dark muddy shadows. Depth is achieved through a combination of crisp tonal layering, refined border articulation, and cool, tinted ambient light.

### Elevation Levels

1. **Flat (Canvas Base / Insets)**:
   - Background: `#f8fafc` or `#f1f5f9`.
   - Shadow: None.
   - Border: 1px solid `#e2e8f0`.

2. **Raised (Standard Resting Cards & Modules)**:
   - Background: `#ffffff`.
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
   - Border: 1px solid rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8).

3. **Floating (Interactive Hover & Secondary Flyouts)**:
   - Background: `#ffffff`.
   - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
   - Accent Tint: Infused with subtle primary cerulean at 6% opacity to simulate medical illumination.

4. **Overlay (Modals, Booking Dialogs, Diagnostic Lightboxes)**:
   - Background: `#ffffff`.
   - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
   - Backdrop: `#0f172a` tinted at 35% opacity with an 8px blur filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`).

## Shapes

The shape system employs balanced geometry (`roundedness: 2`) to ensure interactive components appear inviting and soft while clinical panels remain structurally sound.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Small Elements (Badges, Chips, Micro-inputs)**: `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px) to maintain legibility and crisp bounding edges.
- **Medium Elements (Buttons, Standard Inputs, Select Menus)**: `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px) for comfortable touch targets and balanced hand feel.
- **Large Containers (Cards, Modals, Section Wrappers)**: `rounded-2xl` (1rem / 16px) to eliminate sharp corners that heighten clinical anxiety.
- **Pill Geometry (Status Indicators, Filter Chips)**: Fully rounded (`9999px`) reserved specifically for categorization pills and live availability tags.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#0284c7`, text `#ffffff`, `rounded-lg` (8px). Height: 44px (touch-compliant). Padding: `0 1.25rem`. Hover state transitions to `#0369a1`. Active scale: `0.98`.
- **Secondary**: `#f0fdfa` (Light Mint Teal) fill, `#0d9488` text, 1px border with `#ccfbf1`. Hover state transitions to `#ccfbf1`.
- **Outline / Neutral**: Background transparent, `#334155` text, 1px `#cbd5e1` border. Hover state transitions to `#f8fafc`.

### Cards & Treatment Modules
- Base: White background, 1px `#e2e8f0` outer stroke, 16px corner radius.
- Padding: `1.5rem` internal clearance.
- Interactive Cards (e.g., Selecting an Implant Service or Dental Plan): Include an ambient blue-tinted elevation transition on `:hover` along with an inset 1px stroke shifting to `#38bdf8`.

### Input Fields & Selects
- Structure: 44px height, `#ffffff` fill, 1px `#cbd5e1` border, 8px radius.
- Label: Placed outside top, `label-md` weight, `#334155`.
- Focus State: 1px `#0284c7` border paired with an outer focus ring: `0 0 0 3px rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`. No harsh browser outlines.

### Chips & Trust Badges
- **Clinical Accreditation Badges**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`, background `#f0fdfa`, text `#0f766e`, icon size `14px`.
- **Status Markers**: Dot indicator (6px circle) adjacent to `label-sm` text (e.g., Green `#059669` for "Surgeon Available Today").

### Lists & Step Indicators
- **Pre/Post-Op Instructions**: Numbered steps contained in `#f1f5f9` circular badge containers with `#0284c7` active numerals. Separator lines set to 1px `#e2e8f0`.
- **Checklists**: 20px custom checkbox with `#0d9488` check fills and soft 4px corners.

### Specialized Dental Components
- **Tooth Charting Selector**: Minimalist vector tooth matrix rendered in `#94a3b8` outline, transitioning to `#0284c7` fill with `#e0f2fe` glowing ring when active/selected for treatment planning.
- **Doctor / Surgeon Mini-Profiles**: Circular avatar frame with a 2px `#0d9488` border, accompanied by specialization chips and verified credentials icons.
